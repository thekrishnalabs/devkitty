---

## Selection Rules

A planned tool moves into the product only when it satisfies these conditions:

- It solves a clear and recurring problem
- It fits the existing visual system
- It can be made fast and reliable
- It improves the overall utility of DevKitty
- It is worth maintaining long term

---

## Tool Strategy

The roadmap is not about raw count.
It is about building a complete and trustworthy developer toolbox.
