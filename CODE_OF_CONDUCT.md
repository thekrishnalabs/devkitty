# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

DevKitty is built for developers, contributors, and users who care about quality.

We expect everyone in the project to act with respect, clarity, and professionalism.

### Positive behaviour

- Be respectful
- Be constructive
- Be clear
- Be helpful
- Focus on the work
- Respect different experience levels and viewpoints

### Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment
- Insults
- Personal attacks
- Discrimination
- Trolling
- Deliberate disruption
- Spam
- Abusive language

---

## Project Expectations

- Keep discussions focused on DevKitty.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.
- Disagree with ideas without attacking people.
- Use issue templates and pull request templates where available.
- Respect maintainers’ time.

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or close contributions that violate this code of conduct.

Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from project participation.

---

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to:

- Issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Comments
- Repository-related communication

---

## Final Rule

DevKitty stays professional, calm, and respectful.
